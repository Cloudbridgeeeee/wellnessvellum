How a resource enters the library

  1. Upload leaflets and courses

    Add guides, leaflets, courses and training as PDF, Word or web pages. Each is classified by topic and audience.

  2. Reviewer approval of each leaflet

    A qualified reviewer checks accuracy, tone and reading level, then approves, edits or returns the item.

  3. Library search and gap reports

    Approved items become searchable. Each month a report lists topics people looked for and did not find.

Questions

What education teams ask before a demo.

Ask about a demo

Who is WellnessVellum for?

Organizations that publish adult sexual wellness education: education providers, wellness publishers and healthcare educators. It is not a consumer app.

Who reviews the materials?

Health educators or clinicians on your own team. Each item is published only after their sign-off.

What does it cost?

An organization-level subscription. We quote after a demo, based on your team and library size.

Which files can it read?

PDF, Word documents and web pages, including course outlines and training slides.

Where are our materials kept?

In an encrypted AWS store for your organization alone, deleted on request.
