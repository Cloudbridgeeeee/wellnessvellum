# Product

WellnessVellum: Empower adult wellness education.

AI educational content management software that helps adult sexual wellness organizations classify and recommend professionally reviewed educational materials.

- Founder and CEO: Nathaniel Edeh
- Co-founder and CTO: Amaka Nwosu
- Industry: SexTech, Education, Natural Language Processing
- Customers: Sexual wellness education providers, wellness publishers and healthcare educators
- Core features: Educational content classification, semantic content discovery, knowledge gap analytics, content accessibility tools, professional review workflows
- MVP: A professionally reviewed adult wellness resource library with semantic search
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, S3, OpenSearch, Lambda, DynamoDB
- Business model: Organization-level subscriptions
- Main action: Book a demo
